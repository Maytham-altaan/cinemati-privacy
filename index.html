1 Who we are

Cinemati (سينمائي) is a movie and series trailer app for iOS and Android. It is published by Al-taan LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, USA ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: Maytham.m.aljubori@gmail.com

The short version

  • You can browse and watch trailers without an account. An account is only needed to comment, like, message other users, or report.
  • Your watchlist, loved titles and swipe history stay on your device.
  • AI recommendations are opt-in, and only movie IDs and watch-time figures are sent. Never your name, email or user ID.
  • Ads are requested as non-personalized. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • You can delete your account in the app or by email. See section 9.

2 Information we handle

2.1 Everyone: data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to our servers. The one exception is the limited AI data described in section 4, and only if you opt in.

  • Your watchlist (saved titles) and your loved titles
  • Swipe history: which titles you loved, saved or skipped, and how long you watched each trailer (last 500 swipes)
  • Your selected mood filter and app language
  • Your AI consent choice and a daily AI usage counter
  • A display name you type while not signed in
  • Your sign-in session, if you have an account

You can erase it at any time with Profile → Settings → Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

2.2 If you create an account (optional)

You can sign up with an email address and password, with Google Sign-In, or with Sign in with Apple (iOS). Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service, which keeps your email address, sign-in method and sign-in dates. We never see your password. Firebase stores it in hashed form. From Google or Apple we receive your name, email address and, for Google, a link to your profile photo. With Apple you can choose to hide your real email address.

We then create a profile record in Google Cloud Firestore containing:

  • Your user ID, display name, and search keywords made from your display name (so other users can find you to start a conversation)
  • Your profile photo link (if any) and the date the account was created
  • If you allow notifications: your push token and app language (see 2.4)

Your email address is not part of this profile. (Earlier versions of the app also saved it there. Updated versions remove it the next time you open the app, and we remove any that remain.) Other signed-in users' apps can read your profile record: they show your display name and photo, and use your push token and language only to send you like and reply notifications.

2.3 Things you post or send

  • Public comments and replies on trailers, up to 500 characters, shown with your display name, photo and the time. Every signed-in user can see them. You can delete your own comments at any time by long-pressing them.
  • Likes on trailers and on comments, stored with your user ID. Counts are public.
  • Private messages (1:1 chats): the two participants' user IDs, message text (up to 2,000 characters), timestamps and a preview of the latest message. Our security rules allow only the two participants to read a conversation. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read your messages, except a reported message or conversation when we need to review a report, or where the law requires it.
  • Reports: when you report a user, comment or message, we store your user ID, the reported user's ID, what was reported (comment, message, conversation or title), the reason you typed (up to 500 characters) and the time. Reports are not visible to other users, including the person reported.
  • Blocks: the list of users you have blocked, visible only to you. Blocking hides that user's comments from you and stops messages in both directions.
  • Shares: when you share a trailer we add one to an anonymous total. We do not record who shared.

2.4 Notifications

  • Daily suggestions are scheduled locally on your device from trending titles you have not seen yet. No server is involved.
  • Activity notifications: if you are signed in and allow notifications, the app registers a push token from Expo's push service and saves it, with your app language, on your profile. When another user likes or replies to your comment, their app sends a notification to that token through Expo's push service, which delivers it through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android). The notification contains the other user's display name and, for replies, up to the first 80 characters of the reply.

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

2.5 What we do not collect

No precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number, payment details or health data. The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not build advertising profiles.

  • Keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms of Use, and remove abusive users. (Basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service, and legal obligations.)
  • AI recommendations, only if you opt in. (Basis: your consent.)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for personalized advertising.

4 AI recommendations (Cloudflare Workers AI)

When you first open the app, we ask whether you agree to share viewing data with our AI service. If you choose "Use without AI", nothing is sent and the feature stays off.

If you agree, once you have swiped at least 10 trailers the app uses an open-source AI model (Mistral Small 3.1, run on Cloudflare Workers AI) to rank upcoming trailers for you and to write your one-line "Cinema DNA" description. Each request contains only:

  • Up to 20 TMDB IDs of titles you loved, up to 20 you saved and up to 50 you skipped
  • Your average watch time per trailer, in seconds (ranking only)
  • The IDs of the titles to rank (ranking only), and the language to write in (Cinema DNA only)

Never sent: your name, email, user ID, account details, comments, messages or device identifiers.

Requests go from the app to our own small server on Cloudflare Workers, which runs the model through Cloudflare Workers AI. Our server uses your IP address only to limit request rates. We do not log or store the requests or your IP address. Cloudflare processes each request only to return the answer. Under its Workers AI data usage terms, it does not keep the request or the answer after responding, and it does not use them to train AI models.

The iOS app version 1.0.4 and earlier sent the same requests, through no server of ours, directly to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models (Anthropic's Privacy Policy). Version 1.0.5 and later use Cloudflare as described above.

Changing your mind: go to Profile → Settings → Clear All Data, close and reopen the app, and choose "Use without AI". Clear All Data also erases your watchlist and history on this device, and may sign you out.

5 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request asks for non-personalized ads. Even so, Google's ad SDK collects some data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and prevent fraud:

  • Your device's advertising identifier: the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address, which Google uses to estimate your general location, plus device and app information
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps

On iOS the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) because the ad SDK can use the IDFA. If you decline, Google cannot access it. Either way, we request non-personalized ads.

Controls: Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (wording varies by device). iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7 Security

All connections the app makes use encrypted HTTPS/TLS. Google encrypts Firebase data at rest. Our database security rules let you change only your own profile, comments, likes and blocks, limit each conversation to its two participants, and keep reports write-only for users. We keep collection to what the features need. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9 Delete your Cinemati account

This applies to the Cinemati (سينمائي) app by Al-taan LLC on iOS and Android.

Option A: in the app

  1. Open the Profile tab and tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  2. Under Account & Safety, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete forever.

The app deletes your data listed below right away, then your sign-in account. If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in and repeat the steps. Anything the app could not remove (for example, likes made with older app versions) we remove within 30 days.

Option B: by email (no app needed)

Email Maytham.m.aljubori@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Cinemati account". If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", include your Cinemati display name and we will ask you to verify that the account is yours. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted (within 30 days at most)

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication), including the email address it holds
  • Your profile: display name, photo link, search keywords, push token and language (and any email address an older app version saved there)
  • Your comments and replies, and your likes on trailers and comments
  • Your block list
  • The messages you sent, and any conversation preview showing one of them

What is kept

  • Reports you filed or that were filed about you are kept for safety review for up to 12 months after they are resolved, then deleted. They contain user IDs, what was reported and the report reason, not your profile.
  • Conversations: messages the other person sent you are their content and stay in their chat with you. The conversation record (the two user IDs and dates) is removed within 30 days after both of you have deleted your accounts.
  • Other users' own content: their replies to your comments may still show the display name they replied to, and their block lists may still contain your user ID.
  • Anonymous totals, such as trailer share counts.
  • Anything we must keep to meet a legal obligation.

Account deletion does not touch data stored on your phone. Use Settings → Clear All Data or uninstall the app. Advertising data is held by Google; you can reset your advertising ID as described in section 5. You can also disconnect Cinemati from your Apple ID (iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple) or your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/connections).

Delete some data and keep your account

  • Comments: press and hold your comment, then tap Delete. It is removed right away.
  • Likes: tap the heart again to remove your like.
  • AI taste profile and data on your phone: Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Anything else (for example your profile photo or messages you sent): email Maytham.m.aljubori@gmail.com from the email address on your account and say what to delete. We delete it within 30 days.
